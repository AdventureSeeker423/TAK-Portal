Step 1 of 3

Download the App

  1. iOS users: Search the App Store for TAK Aware and install it.
    TAK Aware is the iOS TAK client. Free download from Apple App Store.
  2. Android users: Search the Google Play Store for ATAK (Android Team Awareness Kit) and install it.
    ATAK is the Android TAK client. Free download from the Play Store.
Step 2 of 3

Scan QR Code to Enroll

Use your phone's camera app to scan the QR code. The code is pre-configured for the {{ORG_NAME}} server and grants access to the {{CHANNEL_NAME}} channel.

  1. Open your phone's Camera app and point it at the QR code on the right.
  2. Tap the notification or link that appears to open it in TAK Aware (iOS) or ATAK (Android). If prompted for Location permission, select Always.
  3. Accept the connection when prompted. Enrollment is automatic — no username or password required.
